# 30 × to Remember

*Some lives are lived once. Some are remembered forever.*

A time-loop dungeon crawler from Kralnu Games. A wizard's spell went wrong and left the town of Aetherfall stuck in time. You remember every loop. Nobody else does.

## [▶ Play the work in progress](https://kralnugames.com/to-remember/)

- **Every action costs time.** Attacks, spells, heals and doors all take seconds off your clock. Beating enemies earns seconds back.
- **The loop always resets.** Your levels stay. Only soulbound gear you're wearing survives.
- **Turn-based fights** with Diablo-style stats (life, damage, armour, crits, dodge), drawn as pixel art straight from code: one sprite per class, coloured for each character.
- **Fallen companions** are lost at the reset, but some come back as strangers. Follow their Distant Echoes to help them remember.
- **The Hamlet:** hire at the Tavern, pick your party at the Campfire, buy supplies at the General Store, bind gear at your Cottage, and spend crystals in the Wizard's Sanctum.

Works offline once installed. Saves stay on your device.

This is an early build. Things will change, and saves may reset between versions.

## Changelog

### 0.11.0 · 14 November 2026
- **Bounties.** The Notice Board posts three new bounties every loop: slay certain creatures, beat elites or a mini-boss, find caches, win fights without anyone falling, or survive long enough. Finish them during a run and collect gold, crystals and sometimes gear when the loop resets.

### 0.10.0 · 14 November 2026
- **Rebuild Bleakthorpe.** A Notice Board in the square lets you spend crystals and gold on projects that survive every reset (and New Game+): the Smithy, a Tavern Extension, a Training Yard, an Apothecary and a Watchtower, each with upgrades.
- **The Smithy.** Rebuild the old forge by the south road, then temper gear up to the wearer's level (Legendaries too), reroll prefixes and suffixes, and upgrade rarity up to Rare, then Epic.
- The forge and the training yard appear on the village map once built.

### 0.9.0 · 13 November 2026
- **The fate reflections.** The loop after the Paradox Regent falls, the Hollow Champion waits in the echoes: the hero as fate intended, built from your own stats, mirroring your two strongest skill-tree paths, copying your moves, and countering any ability one of you uses three times in a row (Perfect Recall).
- The loop after that, the wizard warns you, and then the **Last Rememberer** appears: the wizard who gave up. He uses every milestone boss's signature move, rebuilds your worst loop from the creatures on your Memorial Stone, turns your favourite skill against you, locks your best soulbound item, and charges Thirtyfold Recall, which grows with every loop you've lived.
- **The ending.** Beat the Last Rememberer to free Bleakthorpe. Then start **New Game+** from loop 1, keeping your hero, skill tree, Sanctum, gold, crystals and worn soulbound gear, against enemies 25% tougher. Finish New Game+ and the loop is yours to play freely.
- Two new Legendaries: the Champion's Soul and the Final Memory.

### 0.8.0 · 13 November 2026
- **Milestone bosses.** From loops 5, 10, 15 and 20, a gold door appears once you've survived 20 minutes: the Forgotten King, the Clockwork Sovereign, the Maw Beneath and the Paradox Regent. Each must be beaten before the next appears, and until it falls, creatures from deeper loops stay away.
- Each boss fights its own way: the King's Royal Decree and risen guards, the Sovereign's rewind (Slow stops it) and Overclock, the Maw's grasping tentacles and hunger, and the Regent's shifting forms, Timeline Collapse and second self.
- Time holds its breath at a boss door: the living are healed to full before the fight. One try per run, no smoke bombs.
- **Legendary gear**: the King's Signet, the Sovereign Gear, the Abyssal Heart and the Paradox Crown, each with its own effect. They bind to your hero for good.

### 0.7.0 · 12 November 2026
- **The Nemesis system:** the creature that kills a companion now keeps their true name. When they come back as a stranger and finish their Distant Echoes, a red Nemesis door can appear while they're in your party
- Behind it, the killer waits where your companion fell, and it talks. Beat it and every stranger in your party whose name it holds remembers who they are, and gets their talent
- Lose, run out of time or flee with a Smoke Bomb and it keeps the name: that's a strike, shown as three leaves on the stranger's card. After three strikes it takes pity and gives the name back cursed. They're marked Unworthy: no talent, and a little weaker, forever
- If the whole party falls, the last creature standing holds every lost companion's name. Each win only returns the names of the strangers you brought, so it may need beating more than once
- A Nemesis has one modifier and a fixed boost. It never snowballs
- Companions killed by a mini-boss can't be fully returned: they get their name back, but never their talent
- The Memorial Stone marks the Unworthy with ✕

### 0.5.0 · 11 November 2026

- Earn 1 skill point per hero level, plus bonus points at loops 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30. Reset the tree free once, then for crystals
- Drag, pinch or use + and − to move around the web, and tap a node to learn it
- New hero skills from the tree: **Slow** and **Rewind Turn** (undo the last enemy round)
- **Door hints:** Exploration and Remembrance can show what's behind a door before you open it
- **Pull Time to Heel** (Chronomancy capstone): once per run in the Market Square, the frozen stalls open as a shop and the party heals
- Plus Unbroken, Anchor, Beyond the Loop, Chain Reaction and more. Full list in the design notes

- **Binding:** spend crystals at your Cottage to make a piece of gear soulbound. Each binding costs a little more than the last
- **The Wizard's Sanctum:** permanent crystal upgrades: Borrowed Time, Deep Pockets, Shopkeeper's Favour, Binding Thread and Lucky Purse
